# Acceptance: PRD §12 deliverables and §13 criteria

This file links every deliverable and acceptance criterion of `spec/PRD.md` to its evidence on the final state of Phase 4. Status values:

- **Met** means the evidence exists and was checked.
- **Met (synthetic)** means it was met on the synthetic fixture corpus only.
- **Outstanding** means an input or authorization is missing. Outstanding items are never counted as done.

Test commands are in [`README.md`](README.md#tests). Scenario names refer to `tests/features/*.feature`, which are generated verbatim from the PRD Gherkin.

## Outstanding inputs (owner)

| Item | Effect |
|---|---|
| Anonymized owner corpus, frozen relevance labels for it, and a fidelity sample | All quality, fidelity, and resource numbers come from the synthetic fixture corpus (3 PDFs, 3 images) and synthetic labels. They prove the pipeline, not real medical retrieval quality |
| `OPENAI_API_KEY` and `remote_allowed = true` for a corpus | The OpenAI adapter is implemented and tested against a local HTTP server, and the permission gate is proven in BDD. No live OpenAI run, no OpenAI row in the comparison, and the live integration test `openai_live_embeddings_rank_sensibly` is outstanding |

Low retrieval quality is a valid result (PRD §13). On the synthetic corpus, combined retrieval raises Hit@3 and lowers Precision@3 and nDCG@3 relative to the best text leg; [`phase-4/comparison.md`](phase-4/comparison.md) explains why.
