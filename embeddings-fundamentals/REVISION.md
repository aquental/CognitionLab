# Revision history — adversarial reviews

One table per phase, built from `reports/reviews/phase-N/FINDINGS.md`. Each row is one reviewer finding: who found it, its id and severity, what was wrong, and how it was resolved. A finding marked "reopened" came back after its first fix. The full descriptions, the regression tests, and the reviewer confirmations are in the `FINDINGS.md` of each phase, and the raw reports sit beside it.

Reviewers: Grok (`G`), Codex (`C`), and, from Phase 4, Antigravity (`A`). Every finding was either fixed with a regression test or refuted with evidence, and the reviewer confirmed the outcome.
